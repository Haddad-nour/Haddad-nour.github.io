Project Overview

NovLexPortal is a Full-Stack web platform designed to digitalise and streamline legal litigation management at Assurance Zitouna Takaful.

Problem Statement

Legal teams at Zitouna Takaful managed litigation cases manually across disconnected documents and spreadsheets.

Proposed Solution

NovLexPortal provides a centralised digital platform for the full litigation lifecycle.
